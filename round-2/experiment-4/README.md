## Layman Summary

Tests how well three simple formulas predict the temperature at which water boils at heights up to 4 km, using a standard reference table of water's properties rather than real-world boiling measurements.

## Full Summary

Re-screens three vapour-pressure routes for water boiling temperature over 0-4000 m (50 m steps) against the IAPWS-IF97 saturation curve: constant-latent-heat Clausius-Clapeyron, temperature-dependent-latent-heat Clausius-Clapeyron, and a 3-parameter Antoine fit on 273.15-363.15 K with a held-out band at 363.15-373.15 K (grid points 0-2950 m, 60 points; in-sample is labelled separately). Outputs: full, mini and preview method outputs in exp_gen_sol_out format (81 examples), a grid CSV with in-sample/held-out tags, Clapeyron residuals with the ideal-vapour, Z_v-corrected and Z_v-plus-liquid volumes, independent-curve checks (Antoine and IAPWS-95 derivatives), a sensitivity fit (273.15-353.15 K), and a dataset cross-check against the 100 m equation-derived table. Executed results: constant-L max 0.280 K at 4000 m (RMS 0.153 K); TD-L max 0.176 K (RMS 0.108 K); Antoine full-grid max 0.086 K at sea level (RMS 0.055 K), held-out RMS 0.061 K. Ideal-vapour Clapeyron max residual 1.63 %, Z_v-corrected 0.06 %, independent-curve residuals 0.92 % (Antoine) and 0.06 % (IAPWS-95). Verdict: holds, but largely an identity. Survival (max 0.6 K) holds for all routes. Ranking by held-out RMS: Antoine, TD-L, constant-L. PROVISIONAL: the prior Antoine in-sample target (0.0042 K) did not reproduce (executed 0.0358 K); the Antoine ranking is therefore not settled. No measured boiling records are used; all results are model-to-reference. Headline numbers were re-derived independently from raw CSV/JSON with a brentq reference (audit/rederive.py).
