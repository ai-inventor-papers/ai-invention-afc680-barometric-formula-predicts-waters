## Layman Summary

Checks whether simple altitude formulas predict water's boiling point against measured boiling records. No verified records were found, so the test is untested; only model-only reference numbers are reported.

## Full Summary

This artifact implements a test of two barometric formulas (isothermal and lapse-rate) for the boiling temperature of water against measured station-pressure and boiling-temperature records. The explicit Wagner-Pruss (IAPWS-95) saturation equation is inverted by bisection, with checks at the normal boiling point, the triple point and round trip, and a cross-check against the iapws package (max difference 2.0e-3 K). The pipeline computes residuals, structural and input terms, bootstrap CIs over sites with a fixed seed, and claim verdicts, with a pre-declared holdout and a 15-site minimum. No verified measured records with elevation, station pressure, boiling temperature and stated uncertainty were found, so the accepted set is empty and every claim is marked UNDERPOWERED and untested. The only numbers produced are model-only: the isothermal-minus-lapse-rate structural term on a 100 m grid, 0.332 K at 3000 m and 0.588 K at 4000 m, which an independent re-derivation reproduces. These are not validation. Candidate sources and drop reasons are recorded in data/candidates.csv.
